Today's check-in

Move through each prompt slowly. There are no wrong answers. The goal is to feel it, name it, and find your footing again.

What exactly am I missing today? Be specific. A person, a smell, a street, a routine?
What does this thing I'm missing actually give me? What need is underneath it?
If my mind is catastrophising ("I made a mistake leaving"), what's a truer, kinder sentence?
What's one small way I can recreate a piece of what I'm missing, right where I am?
Name one thing about my life here, today, that I would actually miss if it were gone.
